# Reality Concept 3D — redesign

Visual thesis: a warm, architectural furniture showroom; tactile materials, expressive editorial typography, and one terracotta accent.

Content plan: interactive product hero → room transition → clear service promise → curated live demonstrations → three-step process → store integration → questions → project enquiry.

Interaction thesis: a measured entrance sequence; a sticky object-to-room transition using native scrolling; direct 3D rotation, real fabric variants, and device-aware AR with a desktop QR handoff. Reduced-motion users receive a static sequence.

Demonstration models are labelled as examples, never client projects. No invented metrics, customers, or testimonials.

## Revision: 17 October

Keep the typography, palette and page structure. Replace the extended sticky room transition with one compact, unpinned studio hero. Automatic product rotation is decorative and does not capture pointer or scroll input; visitors explicitly open the full 3D viewer. Motion has a pause control and respects reduced-motion preferences. Upgrade the displayed furniture to textured Poly Haven examples, with provenance retained.
